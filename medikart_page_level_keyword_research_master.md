## 1. Application Restraint & Anti-Spam Limits (Section 3 Enforcement)

- **Meta Title (~60 chars):** Strictly 1, at most 2 primary intent keywords.
- **Meta Description (~155 chars):** 1-2 primary keywords naturally woven into complete informative sentences with value proposition.
- **Headings (H1/H2/H3):** 2-4 keywords naturally reflected in genuine topical headings.
- **Body Copy:** 5-10 contextual entity variations in fluent clinical prose. **ZERO keyword lists or stuffing.**
- **Remaining ~85-90 Keywords:** Serve as the semantic knowledge pool for Answer Engine entity recognition, search auto-complete, and future content roadmaps.
